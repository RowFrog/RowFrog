The app must show these credits wherever their data appears.

| Source | Used for | Licence | Required credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), GeoServer maps.dwd.de | Lake warnings (Binnenseen) and weather warnings per municipality; rain radar and 2-hour nowcast (layer dwd:Niederschlagsradar, read by the app directly) | CC BY 4.0 | "Deutscher Wetterdienst" |

| Österreichischer Ruderverband (rudern.at) | Austrian clubs in the search: names, abbreviations, website, address, state (not the e-mail addresses on the list) | No open licence; imported with the owner's OK, permission to be asked (letter drafted 2026-10-23) | "Österreichischer Ruderverband" |

| Copernicus Land Monitoring Service | Lake Ice Extent 250 m (VIIRS) via Copernicus Data Space, checks the ice estimate on larger lakes | Copernicus data policy: free, full and open | "Copernicus Land Monitoring Service" |
| Windy Webcams API | Webcam images (planned) | Windy API terms | Windy credit and a link on every image |

If the app ever has ads or subscriptions, the Open-Meteo free tier no longer
applies: switch to a paid Open-Meteo plan or load DWD open data directly.
