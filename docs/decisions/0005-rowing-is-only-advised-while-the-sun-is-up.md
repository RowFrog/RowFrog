# 5. Rowing is only advised while the sun is up

- Date: 2026-10-18
- Status: decided (owner)
- Where: `ios/RowFrog/Limits.swift`

## Decision

The rowing light treats daylight as a condition: no green while the sun is down (since 2026-11-06: amber from 60 minutes before sunset, see 14). Air temperature and water temperature are both shown, each with its own symbol.

## Why

Safety.
