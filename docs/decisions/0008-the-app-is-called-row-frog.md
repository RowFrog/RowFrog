# 8. The app is called "Row Frog"

- Date: 2026-10-21
- Status: decided (owner)
- Where: `ios/project.yml`

## Decision

The visible name is "Row Frog" (with a space); bundle ids `com.rowfrog.RowFrog`, scheme `rowfrog://`. The German line is "Der Wetterfrosch fürs Rudern".

## Why

No trademark conflicts found for Row Frog, RowFrog or Ruderfrosch; the domains were free.

## Turned down

RowerWeather and other candidates.
